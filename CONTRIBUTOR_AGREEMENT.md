# Poki Contributor Agreement

Version 1

You agree to this once. It then covers the pull request you agree on and every later contribution you make to this repository.

To agree, comment on your pull request, from your own GitHub account, with this line:

```
I agree to the Poki Contributor Agreement
```

By doing so you confirm, for each of your contributions:

1. **You have the right to submit it.** You wrote it, or you have the right to submit it under the license below. This includes any commits in your pull requests that someone else, or a tool, authored.
2. **You release it under the project license.** Each contribution is released under the [AGPL-3.0-only license](LICENSE). You keep the copyright in your work.
3. **Work made with tools is your work.** A contribution prepared or submitted through your account by a tool, including an AI agent, is your contribution, and you are responsible for it.
4. **It holds no private data.** You removed passcodes, tokens, pairing records, and private screen contents before you submitted it.
5. **Your agreement is on public record.** Your GitHub login, your account id, the date, and a link to your comment are stored in `signatures.json` on the `contributor-signatures` branch of this repository.

If this text changes in a way that matters, the version number goes up and Poki Repo Bot asks you to agree again on your next pull request.
