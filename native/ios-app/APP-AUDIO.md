# Ask about app audio

On iOS 27 or later, open Poki's **Dictation & Mute** settings and tap **Listen to App Audio**. Confirm full-display sharing in the iOS picker. Switch to the app playing the media, then ask about it with Poki's existing voice button. Use **Stop Listening to App Audio** or the system sharing control to end capture.

## What iOS provides

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Can Poki capture audio from another app? | Apple's iOS 27 sample uses a full-display filter that continues across apps. The iOS SDK exposes `SCStreamOutputType.audio`, `capturesAudio`, and `excludesCurrentProcessAudio` for that stream. Poki subscribes to audio only and excludes its own playback. Older iOS versions do not expose this interface. [Apple iOS sample](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/screencapturekit/capturing-screen-content-on-ios), [audio capture configuration](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/screencapturekit/scstreamconfiguration/capturesaudio). |
| Does it work with YouTube, Bilibili, and Safari? | A full-display stream can receive eligible audio while each of those apps is frontmost. Apple does not give a compatibility list for players or titles, and none of those three has been verified on a phone for this change. Test an ordinary YouTube video, an ordinary Bilibili video, and an unprotected Safari video separately. Treat protected audio as unavailable until the target title and player are tested. The old ReplayKit and AVPlayer restriction is evidence about ReplayKit. [Apple iOS sample](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/screencapturekit/capturing-screen-content-on-ios), [Apple FairPlay capture guidance](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/uikit/uiscreen/iscaptured), [Apple ReplayKit overview](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/replaykit). |
| What does the user approve? | Poki supplies `NSScreenCaptureUsageDescription` and opens Apple's full-display sharing picker. The user confirms the share in iOS and can end it with the system sharing control. Poki hides the picker controls for camera and microphone, and receives no microphone or screen output from this stream. The voice button still uses its own microphone permission. The fallback speech recognizer may ask for Speech Recognition permission, and Poki requires local recognition before sending audio to it. [ScreenCaptureKit](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/screencapturekit), [Apple iOS picker sample](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/screencapturekit/capturing-screen-content-on-ios), [Apple Speech authorization](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/speech/asking-permission-to-use-speech-recognition). |
| Can it continue in the background? | Apple documents `screen-capture` for full-display streams while the capture app is in the background. Both Poki editions declare this background mode. The initial question uses audio collected before the voice button takes the microphone. Switching apps, audio interruptions, and playback when the microphone opens still need a physical test. The buffer is lost if iOS ends the app process. [Apple iOS sample](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/screencapturekit/capturing-screen-content-on-ios), [Apple background modes](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/xcode/configuring-background-execution-modes). |
| What changes for Poki Setup? | The feature runs inside Poki's existing app bundle. The usage description and background mode are Info.plist values. Both editions keep their existing extensions and signing inputs, so the feature adds no App ID or provisioning profile. A real update must still verify the original team and app identity. |

## Audio and voice

Poki keeps at most a minute of 16 kHz mono PCM in memory. Stopping or losing the stream clears the buffer. Poki transcribes the snapshot locally using the selected Poki language. Apple may need to download the language asset on first use. Poki sends the transcript to the existing voice connection as quoted material. Phone tools are disabled in that conversation.

The simulator can verify the rolling buffer and voice tool guard with recorded inputs. The iOS 27.0 and 27.3 simulator SDKs lack ScreenCaptureKit. On the iOS 27.2 simulator tested here, DictationTranscriber installed the English and Simplified Chinese assets, but SpeechAnalyzer returned no compatible audio format for either. SpeechTranscriber reported unavailable. SFSpeechRecognizer reported support for local recognition, but the simulator audio probe produced no transcript. The same recorded clip produced a transcript on macOS.

Apple's ScreenCaptureKit sample requires a physical iOS 27 device. On a phone, confirm the system picker, keep Poki in the background while each player runs, and ask what was just said. Repeat with a clip that says to open WeChat and verify that it stays closed. Stop sharing and start a fresh conversation before checking for old audio. Verify an in-place update from a current build signed by Poki Setup before shipping.
